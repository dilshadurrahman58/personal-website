Designing Annotations in Visualization

Considerations from Visualization Practitioners and Educators

Md Dilshadur Rahman, Devin Lange, Ghulam Jilani Quadri, and Paul Rosen

We interviewed ten visualization practitioners about how they design annotations and distilled their practice into six design considerations. Seven visualization educators then reflected on where each one holds and where it breaks down. They are heuristics for reflection, not rules. Below, each one is paired with published charts from The Washington Post, The New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, and The Economist, with notes on where it shows up.
