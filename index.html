About

I’m a postdoctoral researcher in the HIDIVE Lab at Harvard Medical School, working with Nils Gehlenborg.

I earned my PhD in Computer Science from the Kahlert School of Computing, University of Utah, where I worked at the Scientific Computing and Imaging Institute with Paul Rosen. I also hold an MS in Computer Science from the Bellini College of Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity and Computing at the University of South Florida.

My research spans Data Visualization, Human-Computer Interaction, and Computing Education. My work focuses on supporting visualization authoring, annotation design, data communication, and human-AI workflows for visualization creation and understanding.

A broader goal of my research is to understand how people use visualizations and interactive intelligent interfaces for data analysis, comprehension, and communication in interdisciplinary settings. I aim to build user-centered tools that make these tasks more efficient, expressive, consistent, and reliable.

I have also developed educational tools currently used by hundreds of students at major U.S. universities. In addition, I have taught Computer Science in a variety of roles at multiple institutions in Bangladesh and the United States, and I thoroughly enjoy teaching.
